# Spross App Store candidates
iPhone 18 Pro Max (1320×2868) and Apple Watch Ultra 4 49mm (422×514), shot 2026-10-28 on main @ 83c02c7d1; one folder per device model, each file keeps the time it was shot.

★ = the strongest (~10 iPhone, 4 watch). Name scheme: `<source>-<target>_<state>_<screen>`; states: fresh (just onboarded), early (two days, 38 words), grown (a simulated 85-day box of all 1,305 words, 86-day streak, drill Sprossen seeded).

Framing: no card, row or line of text is cut at the bottom edge, and nothing but background or a card's padding sits behind the floating tab bar.
Home's top half (greeting, the day's card) cannot meet that on this phone: its cards stand 24 pt apart and the tab bar zone is 83 pt, so whatever follows the Meadow card is always cut. Home is therefore shown scrolled to its end.
The Box's expanded areas run under the tab bar the same way; the search sheet stands in for them.
